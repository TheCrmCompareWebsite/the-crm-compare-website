Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information CRM Compare (“we,” “us” or “our”) collects when you visit this website, how we use it and the choices you have. The short version

  • No account, no sellingYou don't sign up for anything, and we never sell your personal information.
  • No cookies or trackingNo cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or social media widgets.
  • Search stays on your deviceThe search box on the All Reports page works inside your browser. What you type is never sent to us.
  • Only basic server logsOur web host may keep standard technical logs to run and protect the site.

Part 1 · What we collect

Information you give us

In short: only what you put in an email to us.

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and the contents of your message. We use this only to reply to you and to keep a record of the conversation.

Information collected automatically

In short: your browser sends basic technical details when a page loads, and our host may log them.

When you load a page, your browser sends standard technical information to the server that hosts this site, such as:

  • your IP address, browser type and operating system;
  • the page requested and the page you came from;
  • the date and time of the request.

Our hosting provider, [hosting provider name], may record this in server logs to deliver the site, keep it secure and diagnose problems. We do not combine this information with other data to identify you.

Cookies and tracking

In short: none. We don't track you at all.

This site does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or fingerprinting. We do not respond to “Do Not Track” signals because we do not track visitors to begin with. If we ever add analytics or advertising, we will update this policy first.

Part 2 · What we do with it

How we use information

In short: to run the site, answer you and follow the law. Nothing else.

We use the limited information described above to:

  • operate, maintain and secure the website;
  • respond to messages you send us;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

How we share information

In short: we don't sell or rent it. It only goes to the services that run the site, or where the law requires.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share information only:

  • with service providers, such as our web host and email provider, who process it on our behalf;
  • when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of us or others;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply.

Links to other websites

In short: when you click through to a CRM vendor or another site, their privacy policy applies, not ours.

Our reports link to CRM vendors' websites and to sources such as help centers and review sites. When you follow those links, the other site's privacy policy applies, not ours. [If any links are affiliate links, say so here and name the networks involved.]

In short: only as long as we need it, then it's deleted.

  • Server logs: kept by our hosting provider for [number] days unless needed longer for security.
  • Emails: kept as long as needed to deal with your request, then deleted within [time period].

Security

In short: we take reasonable care, but no website can promise perfect security.

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including serving the site over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Part 3 · Your rights

Your rights and choices

In short: you can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. Just email us.

Depending on where you live, including California and the European Economic Area, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or delete it;
  • object to or restrict how we use it;
  • receive a copy of it.

Because we collect so little, most requests can be handled quickly. To make a request, email [privacy contact email]. We will not treat you differently for exercising these rights. If you are in the EEA or UK, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children's privacy

In short: this site is for real estate professionals, not children.

This site is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Frequently asked questions

What is a real estate CRM?

A CRM (customer relationship management system) keeps every contact, conversation, task and deal in one place. A real estate CRM adds what agents need on top: capturing and routing leads from portals and websites, drip emails and texts, reminders to keep in touch with past clients, and tracking each transaction to closing.

What is the best real estate CRM?

RealtyJuggler has the highest score of the 19 real estate CRMs we scored: 9.8 out of 10. The best one for you depends on how you work, so see our lists for solo agents, teams and more, or every CRM ranked.

How much does a real estate CRM cost?

The typical plans of the 26 CRMs we scored cost from $179 to $11,988 a year; the median is $1,044. The lowest-priced CRM built for real estate is RealtyJuggler, at $179 a year.

Do I need a CRM built for real estate, or will a general CRM do?

19 of the 26 CRMs we scored are built for real estate and 7 are general business CRMs. A general CRM can work, but you have to set up lead routing, transaction tracking and past-client follow-up yourself.

Can I try a CRM before I buy it?

15 of the 26 CRMs we scored offer a free trial. See which trials are longest and which need no credit card.

How do you score each CRM?

Every CRM is scored out of 10 in six categories (features, reviews, value, terms of service, support and governance), and its final score is the average of the six. Read how each category is scored.

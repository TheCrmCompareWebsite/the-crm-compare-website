Frequently asked questions

What is the best CRM for a real estate team?

Wise Agent: of the CRMs rated an ideal fit for high-volume teams, it has the highest final score (8.4 out of 10).

How do you decide whether a CRM fits?

Each report rates the CRM for six types of buyer: a new agent, an average agent, an agent with an assistant, a high-volume team, an office and a franchise. The rating is Ideal, Suitable or a poor fit, from the CRM's features, pricing and who the vendor builds it for.

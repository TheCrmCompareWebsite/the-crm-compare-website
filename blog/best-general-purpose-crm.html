Frequently asked questions

What is the best general-purpose CRM for real estate agents?

Apptivo, with a final score of 7.7 out of 10.

Should I use a real estate CRM or a general CRM?

Real estate CRMs come with lead routing, transaction tracking and past-client tools built in. A general CRM can do the job, but you'd have to set those up yourself. The best general CRM we scored, Apptivo, ranks #3 of 26 overall.

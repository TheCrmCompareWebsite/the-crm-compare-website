CRMs with the Most Protective Data Policies (2026)

Updated November 15, 202612 CRMs, from 26 scored

Your CRM holds your whole client list. These CRMs have the privacy terms that do most to keep that data yours: they say who owns it, promise not to sell or reuse it, and let you take it with you when you leave. At the other end, Real Geeks, CINC and Follow Up Boss scored 5 or less.

How we ranked them. Each report scores the vendor's privacy policy and terms out of 10, starting at 7 and adding or taking off points for data ownership, resale or sharing, AI training, export after leaving and deletion. The article shows every CRM scoring 8 or more, highest first; ties go to the higher governance score.

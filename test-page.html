Always the same layout: logo, product name and company on the left, the score on the right; price per year, price per month, the free trial and the best fit below; a Full Report button across the bottom. Score values

Every score on the site, in one format and one font (Georgia): the score in bold at full opacity, then "/10" lighter and faded; whole numbers have no decimal (9/10, 9.8/10). Coloured by band: green from 7.5, amber from 5.5, red below. Elements may change a score's size, never its font or format.

Made by score() · feature_points() · group_points() in build_site.py · CSS block “score” in style.css

Tables

Every table on the site, in one structure and style: a bordered box, a bold caption, a grey header row in small capitals, and the first cell of each row as the row's heading. Numbers go in "num" cells so they line up on the right, longer notes in "small" cells, and "num win" ticks the better value in a comparison. On a phone each row becomes a small card with every value labelled.

The six category scores on six axes, Features at the top and then clockwise, with rings at 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10. Drawn on the page, not an image: the rings, axes and labels are CSS and the shape is a small inline SVG, all styled in style.css. The shape takes the colour of the final score's band.

Fit chart

How well a CRM fits each of six types of buyer, as a wheel of six segments (Ideal green, Suitable amber, Poor red) around the CRM's name and final score, with a ring showing the score. Drawn with CSS only. The ratings are "fit_ratings" in each CRM's data file.

Made by fit_chart() in build_site.py · CSS block “fit-chart” in style.css
